    How it works

    Five steps, from your classroom to London

    You enter the group. We provide the syllabus, the platform and a coordinator alongside you.

    1

    Registration

    You enter your school and your students in their age category and subject.

    2

    Preparation

    You receive the official resources and syllabus for English or Maths for each category.

    3

    Online round

    Your students sit the paper online from school, on the official round date. 30 questions in 60 minutes, across three difficulty levels, on a laptop, tablet or phone.

    4

    Results

    Each student receives their international certificate according to the score they achieved.

    5

    Grand Final

    The highest scorers advance to the regional final and to the Grand World Final in London.

    Categories

    Three categories, ages 9 to 17

    Each category has its own syllabus and difficulty level, in both English and Maths.

    🐝

    Little Bee

    Ages 9 to 11

    Upper primary. Content pitched to the age group, focused on comprehension and reasoning.

    🐝

    Honeybee

    Ages 12 to 14

    Lower secondary. More demanding on vocabulary, grammar and mathematical problem solving.

    🐝

    Bumblebee

    Ages 15 to 17

    Upper secondary. The highest level of the competition, and the shortest route to the World Final prizes.

    Frequently asked questions

    What teachers ask

    Which subjects can my group enter?

    English and Maths. You can enter your students in one subject or in both, in the category that matches their age.

    Where is the online round sat?

    In your own school, on the official round date. All you need is computers or tablets with an internet connection. No student has to travel to a venue.

    Which countries can take part?

    Schools anywhere in the world can enter. Students from more than 30 countries already take part, and the online round is sat in your own school wherever you are.

    How many students can I enter?

    There is no minimum. You can enter one group, a whole year or the entire school. Tell us the approximate number on the form and a coordinator will talk it through with you.

    What does it cost to take part?

    There is no cost to request the information or to register your school. The entry fee per student depends on the round and the category; a coordinator shares the full detail by WhatsApp before you make any decision.

    Is travelling to London compulsory?

    No. The online round and the international certificate are entirely independent of the trip. Attending the Grand World Final is optional and only for students who qualify.

    When is the next round?

    The next online round is on 23 and 24 October 2026, and the full calendar above runs through to April 2027. Leave your details and a coordinator will send the official calendar and syllabus by WhatsApp.

    What do I get as a teacher?

    Recognition of your school as a participating institution, the official preparation materials, and a coordinator alongside you throughout the process.
